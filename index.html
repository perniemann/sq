sq_ - browser 3D squash

About

sq_ is a browser-only 3D squash game: WSF-dimension court, Rapier physics, black/white court with cyan/orange accents, two-button input, and PARS-11 best-of-3 scoring. No account and no backend — play at https://sq.perniemann.com/.

Controls

Space or the right touch zone charges and releases a shot. Shift or the left touch zone chases the ball. Aim with A/D or drag while charging.
